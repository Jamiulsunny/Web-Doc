Culinary Philosophy

An Instinctive Dialogue With Nature

Located at 3 Rue de Prague in the 12th arrondissement of Paris, Table is not a conventional restaurant. It is a wave-shaped sanctuary where the division between chef and guest dissolves entirely.

Bruno Verjus crafts an ephemeral menu determined exclusively by daily deliveries from artisanal growers, free divers, and regenerative farmers. Ingredients arrive still vibrating with life.

Visionary Craftsman

Bruno Verjus

Physician, food critic, and author, Bruno Verjus chose to step into the professional kitchen at age 54. He brought with him a profound biological understanding of digestion, terroir, and the raw poetry of real sustenance.

"Cooking is not transforming. It is liberating the inner essence of what has grown under the sun."
