The Table Experience

Spatial Design

The Curved Pewter Counter

"No barriers. No culinary secrecy. The guest is invited into the heart of the culinary dance."

The restaurant's defining architectural element is its custom-crafted pewter counter. Instead of isolated tables, guests sit along this fluid ribbon. This layout transforms service into an ongoing conversation between the chefs, sommeliers, and diners.

You witness the precise slicing of fish, the scent of charcoal as crustaceans touch the grill, and the gentle pour of reductions moments before tasting.

Cellar Selections

Living Wines

Our wine philosophy mirrors the kitchen: strictly organic, biodynamic, and low-intervention bottles from passionate vignerons who allow terroir to speak without chemical interference.

Culinary Rhythm

The Unhurried Pace

A meal at Table is an immersive performance lasting three to four hours. We ask guests to leave external schedules at the door and tune into the natural rhythm of the service.

Tactile Details

Artisanal Objects

From hand-forged knives by Japanese master bladesmiths to bespoke ceramics from local French potters, every tactile point has been thoughtfully selected.
