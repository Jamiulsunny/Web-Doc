DIVISION 01 Collegiate Academic Division

Division of Humanities

The Humanities Division at Oxford unites global scholarship across eleven faculties, the Ruskin School of Art, the Faculty of Music, and historic linguistic archives.

📚 Core Research Areas

  • Philosophy of Mind & Logic: Epistemology, ethical AI frameworks, metaphysics, and classical Greek philosophy.
  • Classical Antiquity & Epigraphy: Decipherment of Mediterranean papyri, archaeological heritage conservation.
  • Comparative Global Literature: Medieval vernaculars, post-colonial narrative studies, Renaissance poetics.
  • Theology & Cultural Religions: Interfaith discourse, historical theology, and moral philosophy.

Degree Pathways

BA Philosophy, Politics & Economics (PPE) BA Literae Humaniores (Classics) MPhil Historical Studies DPhil Medieval Linguistics

⚖ Flagship Ongoing Projects

Active Digital Project Archive 2024–2028

The Multispectral Manuscript Recovery Initiative

Optical infrared and ultraviolet scanning uncovering erased marginal palimpsests in the Bodleian collection.

Ethics Center Ongoing Chair

Institute for Ethics in AI

Embedding moral philosophy, justice, and human rights into algorithmic policy development.

Key Milestone: Awarded multiple European Research Council Advanced Grants for historic linguistic preservation.
DIVISION 02 Translational Medical Division

Division of Medical Sciences

Consistently recognized globally for translational pharmacology, clinical trials, vaccine engineering, structural genomics, and neuroscience.

🔬 Core Research Areas

  • Vaccine Development & Immunology: Viral-vector platforms, transmission-blocking antigen formulations.
  • Structural Genomics & Proteomics: High-throughput crystal structures for target discovery.
  • Neuroscience & Psychiatric Biology: Deep brain stimulation, neurodegenerative mitigation strategies.
  • Translational Oncology: Radiotherapy targeting, early liquid biopsy biomarkers for cell carcinomas.

Degree Pathways

BM BCh Clinical Medicine BA Medical Sciences MSc Global Health Science & Epidemiology DPhil Clinical Neurosciences

⚖ Flagship Ongoing Projects

Clinical Platform Global Trial Protocol

Next-Generation Malaria & Viral Vector Vaccines

Collaborative sub-Saharan clinical trials advancing high-efficacy R21 transmission blockers.

Bio-Data Platform Continuous Dataset

Deep Genomic Characterization of Autoimmunity

Sequencing single-cell RNA transcriptomes to anticipate patient responses to targeted biologic therapies.

Clinical Benchmark: Over 15 active Phase III clinical trials operating across hospitals in the Oxford University Hospitals NHS Foundation Trust.
DIVISION 03 Scientific & Computational Division

Mathematical, Physical & Life Sciences Division (MPLS)

Pioneering theoretical and empirical physics, quantum computing architectures, pure mathematics, robotics, and planetary biology.

🔮 Core Research Areas

  • Quantum Information & Nanotechnology: Trapped-ion quantum gates, solid-state qubits, quantum cryptography.
  • Astrophysics & Cosmology: Dark matter modeling, deep interferometry telescope arrays, black hole mechanics.
  • Mathematical Institute: Differential geometry, number theory, mathematical biology, and fluid dynamics.
  • Autonomous Robotics & Machine Vision: Safe reinforcement learning, spatial localization in GPS-denied environments.

Degree Pathways

MMath Mathematics MPhys Physics BA Computer Science DPhil Quantum Information & Devices

⚖ Flagship Ongoing Projects

Quantum Hub Multi-Faculty

Trapped-Ion High-Fidelity Quantum Processors

Engineering photonic interconnects between ion traps for room-temperature quantum computing networks.

Planetary Systems Atmospheric Lab

The Environmental Change Institute (ECI) Carbon Registry

Empirical planetary satellite analysis calibrating ocean carbon capture rates with global sea temperature shifts.

Historic Milestone: Home to the Mathematical Institute at the Andrew Wiles Building, fostering international Fields Medal collaborations.
DIVISION 04 Societal & Policy Division

Division of Social Sciences

The largest grouping of social scientists in the UK, informing international public policy, macroeconomic modeling, jurisprudence, and refugee protection.

⚗ Core Research Areas

  • International Law & Human Rights: Public international law, humanitarian treaties, comparative judicial policy.
  • Applied Macroeconomics: Monetary economics, income disparity measurement, global trade equilibria.
  • Global Governance & Public Policy: Policy evaluation through the Blavatnik School of Government.
  • Forced Migration & Refugee Studies: Humanitarian law, border sociology, displaced person assimilation.

Degree Pathways

BA Jurisprudence (Law) BA Economics & Management Master of Public Policy (MPP) DPhil International Relations

⚖ Flagship Ongoing Projects

Policy Lab Global Tracking

The Oxford Government Policy & Containment Monitor

Tracking global institutional responses to climate migration, pandemics, and energy inflation across 180+ nations.

Economics Forum Annual Policy Series

The Oxford Poverty & Human Development Initiative (OPHI)

Formulating the Multidimensional Poverty Index adopted by the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP).

Policy Authority: Direct advisor to multilateral institutions including the World Bank, International Court of Justice, and OECD.
