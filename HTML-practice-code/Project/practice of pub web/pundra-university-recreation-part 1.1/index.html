About The University

The Pundra University of Science & Technology is proud to be the first Private University in North Bengal aiming to provide high quality higher education at affordable cost at the door step of the young, matured and marginalized people. It was established under the Private University Act with the approval of the Ministry of Education.

Our Campus Has Best Facilities For Our Students

  1. Highly qualified full-time faculty members

  3. English proficiency development program
  4. Computing skill development program
  5. Rich library collections with books and journals and on-line access to e-learning resources
  6. Formal consulting/tutorial sessions, and make-up classes to alleviate students' learning difficulties
  7. Student counselling, career guidance and placement services
