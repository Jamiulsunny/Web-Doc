Message from Head

Md. Habib Ehsanul Hoque

Head, Dept. of CSE

The Department of Computer Science & Engineering (CSE), Pundra University of Science & Technology (PUB) is one of the leading departments in this University. The curriculum has been designed in accordance with premier universities around the world.

Our classrooms and laboratories are equipped with modern teaching-learning facilities and experimental kits for students.

Vision

The Department believes that student learning is the top priority and we work hard to offer our students a high standard of engineering education to achieve their fullest potential.

Mission
  1. To provide high quality state of the art education and knowledge in Computer Science and Engineering.
  2. To contribute towards the creation of new knowledge through eminence research.
  3. To enhance Industry-Institute Interaction programs to get acquainted with corporate culture.
Programs
  • B.Sc. in CSE (161.00 Credits, 8 Semesters)
  • B.Sc. in CSE (For Diploma)
