About the University

The Pundra University of Science & Technology is proud to be the first Private University in North Bengal aiming to provide high quality higher education at affordable cost at the door step of the young, matured and marginalized people. It was established under the Private University Act with the approval of the Ministry of Education.

The Honorable President of the People's Republic of Bangladesh is the Chancellor of the Pundra University of Science & Technology. The University provides a congenial learning environment along with available modern facilities, applies student-centered creative and interactive learning methods, pursues applied research, fosters collaborations to produce world standard graduates to meet national and global challenges.

The Pundra University of Science & Technology is a non-profit private higher educational institution contributed to build a knowledge-based society of empowering people for quality of life.

The University is Committed to
  • Provide best quality learning experiences with comprehensive knowledge and skill based education and emphasize on their professional development that empowers students to cultivate, innovate and navigate their career disciplines.
  • Encourage faculty members to conduct applied research to address important scientific, business, socio-political and cultural questions of the new century.
  • Nurture human excellence, integrated moral and cultural values in order to promote goodness and dignity of every individual.
Institutional Identity

Academic freedom for excellence will require the University to attract best faculty and efficient management through forging relationships with national and international community of sister organizations.
