WHY CHOOSE US

Choosing Pundra University of Science and Technology (PUB) might be influenced by several unique factors and benefits that the university offers. Here are some reasons why one might choose PUB:

1. Academic Programs
  • Offers a wide range of programs in science, technology, engineering, and related fields.
  • Industry-aligned curriculum with a focus on practical skills and emerging technologies.
2. Research Opportunities
  • Strong emphasis on research and innovation with well-funded projects.
  • Opportunities for undergraduate and graduate students to work in research labs.
  • Collaborations with national and international research institutions.
3. Faculty Expertise
  • Highly qualified professors with PhDs from reputed universities.
  • Mentors who support student projects and career growth.
4. Modern Facilities
  • State-of-the-art labs and high-speed campus-wide WiFi network.
  • Smart classrooms with digital learning tools.
  • Rich physical and e-library resources.
5. Industry Connections
  • Strong ties with top industries for internships and placements.
  • Frequent seminars and industry guest lectures.
6. Campus Environment

A serene, green, and secure campus located in Gokul, Bogura.

7. Location

Easily accessible on Rangpur Road in North Bengal.

8. Affordability

Affordable tuition fees with up to 90% scholarships and financial waivers.

9. Accreditation & Recognition

Fully approved by the Ministry of Education and UGC Bangladesh.

10. Alumni Success

Graduates placed in top MNCs, government organizations, and higher studies abroad.

11. International Exposure

Collaborations with international universities for faculty and student exchange.
