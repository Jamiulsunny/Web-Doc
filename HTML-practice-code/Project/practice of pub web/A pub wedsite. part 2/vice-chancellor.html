Message from the Vice-Chancellor

Prof. Dr. Chitta Ranjan Misra

Vice-Chancellor
Pundra University of Science & Technology

Pundra University of Science & Technology is trying to promote excellence in higher education for a vibrant and inclusive society through knowledge creation and dissemination. University is making sincere effort to contribute its mite by providing the right kind of human resources.

We are striving hard to impart quality education to meet national and global challenges towards accomplishing our mission. The University has highly qualified and young faculty in their respective fields of expertise situated in a serene green campus.

I have great pleasure in inviting students to join the family of academic fraternity. Pundra University is always proud of our students' attainments.
