Message from the Chairman, BoT

Ashoka Fellow & PHF

Prof. Dr. Hosne-Ara Begum

Chairman, Board of Trustees (BoT) · Pundra University of Science & Technology

Welcome to Pundra University of Science and Technology (PUB), the pioneer private university in North Bengal. From its inception, our unwavering mission has been to enlighten lives through science, modern technology, and humane ethics. We envisioned an institution where students from every corner of the country, especially those from marginalized backgrounds, could access world-class higher education without socio-economic boundaries.

In today's fast-paced digital era, academic institutions must transcend beyond traditional textbook boundaries. At PUB, our research-driven classrooms, outcome-based curricula, and cutting-edge laboratory facilities nurture creative thinkers, future-ready engineers, and compassionate leaders. I warmly invite all students to embark on an enriching academic voyage with us.

Prof. Dr. Hosne-Ara Begum
Founder & Executive Director, TMSS
Chairman, Board of Trustees, Pundra University
