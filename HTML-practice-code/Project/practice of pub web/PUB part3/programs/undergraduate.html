Undergraduate Academic Programs (Bachelor's Degrees)

Embark on your journey toward intellectual leadership, innovative technology, and professional success with our UGC-approved 4-year undergraduate degrees designed to meet the demands of global industries.

UGC & BAETE Aligned

Outcome-Based Education (OBE) curriculum.

Advanced Modern Labs

Specialized hardware, networking & engineering labs.

Up to 100% Scholarship

Generous merit waivers for GPA 5.0 and needy students.

Co-Curricular Growth

Coding club, robotics society, sports & moot court.

Available Bachelor's Degree Programs

Science & Engineering

B.Sc. in Computer Science & Engineering (CSE)

Duration: 4 Years (8 Semesters) Credits: 160.00

Cutting-edge education in AI, Machine Learning, Software Engineering, Algorithms, Database Systems, Computer Networks, and Cyber Security.

Department Apply Now
Evening / Weekend Track

B.Sc. in CSE (For Diploma Engineers)

Duration: Flexible Track Eligibility: Diploma in Engineering

Specially customized for polytechnic diploma graduates looking to upgrade their academic degree to a full Bachelor of Science while working.

Department Apply Now
Science & Engineering

B.Sc. in Electrical & Electronic Engineering (EEE)

Duration: 4 Years (8 Semesters) Credits: 160.00

Equips students in power systems, renewable energy, electronics, telecommunications, robotics, control systems, and VLSI circuit design.

Department Apply Now
Science & Engineering

B.Sc. in Civil Engineering (CE)

Duration: 4 Years (8 Semesters) Credits: 162.00

Focusing on modern structural design, geotechnical surveying, highway & transportation planning, and environmental water resource management.

Department Apply Now
Faculty of Business Studies

Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA)

Duration: 4 Years (8 Semesters) Credits: 126.00

Four distinct majors: Finance & Banking, Marketing, Accounting & Information Systems, and Human Resource Management (HRM).

Department Apply Now
Faculty of Law

Bachelor of Laws (LL.B Honours)

Duration: 4 Years (8 Semesters) Credits: 132.00

Comprehensive legal training approved by Bangladesh Bar Council, featuring mock trial practice in modern moot courts, jurisprudence, and civil laws.

Department Apply Now
Humanities & Social Sciences

B.A. (Hons) in English

Duration: 4 Years (8 Semesters) Credits: 124.00

Rich curriculum blending classic and modern English literature, phonetics, linguistics, communicative ELT training, and creative writing.

Department Apply Now
Humanities & Social Sciences

B.A. (Hons) in Islamic Studies

Duration: 4 Years (8 Semesters) Credits: 124.00

In-depth exploration of Quranic sciences, Hadith literature, Islamic civilization, jurisprudence (Fiqh), Arabic language, and moral philosophy.
