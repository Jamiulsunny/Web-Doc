Postgraduate Academic Programs (Master's Degrees)

Elevate your professional career and analytical leadership through our UGC-approved Master's degrees, tailored with flexible weekend and evening schedules for working professionals and graduates.

Weekend & Evening

Friday-Saturday classes designed for service holders.

Experienced Faculty

Professors & PhD holders from public universities.

Affordable Fees

Easy semester installment facility & special waivers.

OBE Research Focus

Practical thesis, case studies & field analytics.

Available Graduate Degrees

Business Administration

Master of Business Administration (MBA)

Duration: 2 Years Credits: 60 Shift: Evening/Friday

Designed for non-business graduates seeking foundational and strategic business acumen in Marketing, Finance, Accounting, and HRM.

Curriculum Apply Now
Accelerated Track

MBA (1-Year Program)

Duration: 1 Year Credits: 36 Eligibility: 4-Year BBA Holders

Accelerated advanced business management degree for BBA graduates looking to specialize in corporate finance, strategic marketing, or leadership.

Curriculum Apply Now
Executive Track

Executive MBA (EMBA)

Duration: 16 Months Credits: 48 Shift: Fridays Only

Specially curated for working corporate executives, bankers, NGO managers, and government officials to sharpen strategic decision-making.

Curriculum Apply Now
Public Health

Master of Public Health (MPH)

Duration: 1.5 - 2 Years Credits: 51 Shift: Weekend

Empowering medical practitioners, nurses, and healthcare executives in epidemiology, hospital management, healthcare policy, and biostatistics.

Curriculum Apply Now
Faculty of Humanities

M.A. in Islamic Studies (1Y & 2Y)

Duration: 1 / 2 Years Credits: 36 / 64 Shift: Flexible

Advanced studies in Quranic hermeneutics, Islamic jurisprudence (Fiqh), ethical philosophy, and comparative theological history.

Faculty of Law

Master of Laws (LL.M)

Duration: 1 Year (General) Credits: 36 Shift: Evening/Friday

Advanced legal analysis, constitutional rights, international corporate law, cyber law, and judicial service training for law graduates.

Faculty of Humanities

M.A. in English (ELT & Literature)

Duration: 1 / 2 Years Credits: 36 / 64 Shift: Weekend/Evening

Comprehensive graduate focus in English Language Teaching (ELT), applied linguistics, critical theory, and world literary history.
