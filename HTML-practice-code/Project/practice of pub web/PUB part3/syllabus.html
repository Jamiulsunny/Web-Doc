OBE Curriculum & Academic Syllabus

Outcome-Based Education (OBE) Framework

In accordance with the guidelines of the University Grants Commission (UGC) of Bangladesh and the Board of Accreditation for Engineering and Technical Education (BAETE), Pundra University has implemented a learner-centric Outcome-Based Education (OBE) curriculum across all undergraduate and postgraduate programs.

1. PEOs (Program Educational Objectives)

Broad statements describing career and professional accomplishments that graduates achieve within a few years after graduation.

2. PLOs / POs (Program Outcomes)

Specific knowledge, analytical skills, and ethical values students possess upon successful completion of the academic degree.

3. CLOs (Course Learning Outcomes)

Measurable learning outcomes that students are expected to demonstrate at the end of each specific course unit.

4. Continuous Assessment

Formative rubrics, projects, presentations, and lab viva verifying progressive achievement of competencies.
