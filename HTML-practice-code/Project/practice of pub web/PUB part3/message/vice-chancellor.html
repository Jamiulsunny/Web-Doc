Welcome from the Vice-Chancellor

It is an honor and a distinct privilege to welcome you to Pundra University of Science & Technology (PUB), Bogura. As the chief academic and administrative officer, I take immense pride in steering an institution that continuously strives for excellence in teaching, innovative research, and meaningful societal development.

“A university is not merely a place of lectures and grades; it is a vibrant crucible where intellectual curiosity meets human compassion to solve the challenges of tomorrow.”

At Pundra University, we believe that education must evolve in tandem with the Fourth Industrial Revolution (4IR). To meet global standards, we have meticulously modernized our curricula into an Outcome-Based Education (OBE) model across all disciplines—including Computer Science & Engineering, Electrical & Electronic Engineering, Civil Engineering, Business Administration, Law, and the Humanities.

OBE Framework Outcome-Based Learning
Research Culture Indexed Publications
Industry Tie-ups Internships & Placements

Our distinguished faculty members, state-of-the-art computer and engineering laboratories, digital library facilities, and interactive learning environments are structured to provide each student with personalized guidance. Beyond the classrooms, we inspire active participation in competitive programming, sports tournaments, cultural celebrations, and social outreach initiatives.

To all our prospective and current students: I encourage you to dream courageously, think critically, and dedicate yourselves wholeheartedly to your chosen fields. Together, let us cultivate a campus that radiates knowledge, integrity, and sustainable progress.

Prof. Dr. Chitta Ranjan Misra
Vice-Chancellor
Pundra University of Science & Technology, Bogura, Bangladesh
