Message from the Treasurer

It is an honor to extend warm greetings on behalf of the Office of the Treasurer at Pundra University of Science and Technology (PUB). Higher education is one of the most vital investments an individual and a nation can undertake. Our core fiscal mission is to ensure that quality education remains affordable, transparent, and completely free from unjustifiable financial barriers.

“Prudent financial planning combined with transparent governance guarantees that every resource directly enriches the student learning experience and research capabilities.”

At PUB, we have instituted a balanced, student-centric financial framework. We ensure that tuition fees remain among the most competitive in the country while continuously expanding state-of-the-art computer and engineering laboratories, campus digital services, and our central library holdings.

100% Transparent Digital Accounting
BDT 2 Cr+ Annual Research & Lab Budget
Up to 100% Merit & Need-Based Waivers

Under the generous policy of our Board of Trustees, billions of Takas are allocated in the form of scholarships, sibling discounts, female student stipends, and waivers for meritorious and economically underprivileged students. We believe that no deserving student should be deprived of higher education due to financial hardship.

With robust internal controls, modern digital UIMS payment gateways, and transparent audits, we pledge our continued support toward making Pundra University a world-class center of knowledge and innovation.

Md. Aminul Islam Sarker
Treasurer
Pundra University of Science & Technology, Bogura
