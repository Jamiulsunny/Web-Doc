CHRONOLOGY OF DISCOVERY

JOURNEY THROUGH SPACE

From humanity's earliest orbital probes to the upcoming frontier on Mars.

1957

First Artificial Satellite

Sputnik 1 was launched into low Earth orbit, inaugurating the Space Age and human satellite engineering.

1969

First Humans on the Moon

Apollo 11 touches down in the Sea of Tranquility, realizing the historic milestone of human steps on another celestial body.

1990

Hubble Space Telescope

Deployed above Earth's atmospheric distortion, transforming our visual perception of deep galaxies and star nurseries.

1997

Mars Pathfinder

The Sojourner rover landed on Mars, proving that lightweight semi-autonomous vehicles could navigate planetary terrain.

2021

Perseverance on Mars

Touchdown in Jezero Crater to extract geological samples and test the Ingenuity Mars helicopter in thin atmosphere.

FUTURE

Human Exploration of Mars

Crewed orbital transfer, permanent habitat domes, and humanity evolving into a multi-planetary species.
