Homemade Pizza Recipe

Published: 3 October, 2026

Ingredients :

List for pizza

  • Pizza Dough - 1 large
  • Cheese - 200g
  • Tomatoes - 2 medium
  • Onion - 1 large
  • Capsicum - 1
  • Chicken - 250g
  • Pizza Sauce - 4 tbsp
  • Oregano and Chili Flakes - 1 tsp

Instructions

  1. Prepare the pizza dough and roll it into a round shape.
  2. Spread pizza sauce evenly over the dough.
  3. Add chicken, onion, tomatoes, and capsicum.
  4. Sprinkle grated cheese over the toppings.
  5. Add oregano and chili flakes.
  6. Bake the pizza until the cheese melts and the crust becomes golden brown.
  7. Slice the pizza and serve hot.
