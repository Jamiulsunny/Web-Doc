Institutional Overview

Pundra University of Science & Technology (PUB) was established in 2001 under the Private University Act to serve as a beacon of modern technological and liberal arts education in the northern region of Bangladesh. Located beside the historic Mahasthangarh in Bogura, PUB combines regional heritage with cutting-edge academic curricula.

Our Vision

To be recognized nationally and internationally as a center of scientific innovation, intellectual discovery, and social progress that prepares ethical global citizens.

Our Mission

To impart quality education across sciences, technology, business, and legal studies through experiential learning, faculty development, and community impact.

The Board of Trustees

The university is governed by a distinguished Board of Trustees consisting of renowned academics, industrialists, and social advocates dedicated to advancing education in North Bengal.
